What Is Communication?

Communication is the process of sharing ideas, information, thoughts, and feelings. It can be verbal, nonverbal, written, or visual.

Verbal

Spoken words and conversations

Nonverbal

Body language, gestures, tone

Written

Texts, letters, notes, emails

Visual

Images, signs, charts, symbols

Tips for Effective Communication

1
Listen activelyGive full attention and show you understand.
2
